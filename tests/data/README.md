# Test data

`face.jpg`: the head from Gilbert Stuart's *Portrait of George Washington* (the
Williamstown portrait, 1798), cropped and downscaled. The painting is in the public
domain; source: [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Gilbert_Stuart_Williamstown_Portrait_of_George_Washington.jpg).

The face is painted turned about 20 degrees to one side, so its mirror image is the same
head turned about 40 degrees from it: the end-to-end test uses the pair as "looking at
the screen" and "looking away". No photos of real rooms or people are kept here.
